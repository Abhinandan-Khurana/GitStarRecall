# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

GitStarRecall is a local-first, security-focused open source project. We want participation here to be respectful, constructive, and safe for contributors, maintainers, and users.

By participating in this project, you agree to:
- be respectful in disagreement and assume good intent before escalating,
- give technical feedback that is specific, evidence-based, and focused on the work,
- welcome contributors with different backgrounds, skill levels, and perspectives,
- respect personal, professional, and cultural boundaries,
- prioritize the safety and dignity of other community members.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commits, and any related community channels:
- harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention,
- hateful or demeaning language, slurs, or personal attacks,
- threats of violence or encouragement of self-harm,
- deliberate disruption, dogpiling, or bad-faith engagement,
- publishing or threatening to publish someone else's private information without permission,
- repeated attempts to bypass community boundaries after being asked to stop,
- conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and whenever someone is representing GitStarRecall in a public context. That includes repository discussions, pull requests, issue comments, review threads, and linked community spaces run for this project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to [l0u51f3r007@gmail.com](mailto:l0u51f3r007@gmail.com).

When possible, include:
- what happened,
- where it happened,
- when it happened,
- links, screenshots, or other supporting context,
- whether there is an immediate safety concern.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Information will be shared only with people who need it to investigate and respond.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for setting and enforcing community standards fairly and consistently. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers should:
- focus on protecting participants and restoring a safe working environment,
- evaluate reports based on behavior and impact, not contributor status,
- avoid public disclosure of private reports beyond what is necessary to act,
- communicate decisions and next steps as clearly as the situation allows.

## Enforcement

When a violation is confirmed, maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate, including:
- a private warning,
- a request to stop specific behavior,
- removal of content,
- temporary suspension from project spaces,
- permanent ban from participation.

Serious threats, targeted harassment, doxxing, or patterns of abusive behavior may result in immediate removal without prior warning.
